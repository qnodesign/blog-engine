## One System, Endless Possibilities

Everyone has seen this: one outlet, one adapter, then another adapter on top of it, and another, until the wall looks like a sculpture. Every new device makes it worse. It works, until it doesn't, and then nobody knows which part to blame.

Konihaus is built the opposite way. Think of the classic Swiss army knife: one tool, many functions, everything in its place. Now picture a clean distributor with an endless number of sockets. Whatever you need today, you plug in. Whatever you need tomorrow, you add. Nothing gets stacked, nothing gets tangled, and nothing has to be replaced to make room.

  <img src="/images/insights/swiss-quality/endless-sockets.webp" alt="A clean socket distributor with room for endless connections" width="1536" height="1024" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

That is how we think about a smart home:

- **Easy to extend.** New lights, shades, sensors, or heating components join the existing system without starting over.
- **Easy to understand.** You should be able to see what is connected and how it works, without needing a manual or a specialist.
- **One home, one system.** Not ten apps and ten accounts, but a single place where everything comes together.

## Smart Home in Switzerland: Familiar With Swiss Living

Switzerland does many things differently from its neighbours, and the differences go well beyond the outlet. The Type J socket is the one everybody notices. Behind the wall there is more: how switches are wired, which electrical cables are used, how the fuse box is organised. Even furniture follows its own measures: a kitchen appliance is typically 55 cm wide here, not the 60 cm common elsewhere.

None of this is new to us. We know these details and plan around them. When something needs to be adapted, whether a device from abroad, an installation, or a plan, we point it out and guide you through it.

- **In the wall.** Switch wiring, cable types, and the layout of the fuse box.
- **At the socket.** Type J, planned with the right components from the start rather than patched with adapters.
- **In the room.** Furniture and appliance dimensions, such as 55 cm in the kitchen.
- **On paper.** Cantonal regulations can differ from canton to canton. We pay attention to the requirements that apply to your home and your location.

## Regulations Are Monitored and Handled

Swiss quality goes beyond sockets and measurements. Regulations change, and someone has to keep track of them.

We monitor and handle them for you. That includes the technical standards that apply here, but also imported products: a device that was designed for another market has to be checked before it belongs in a Swiss home. Quality checks of software and services are carried out here in Switzerland, to the highest standards we can apply.
